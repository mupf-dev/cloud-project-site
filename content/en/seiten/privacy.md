## Principle

This website sets **no cookies**, uses **no tracking and no analytics tools**
and loads **no third-party resources** (no external fonts, scripts or embeds).
Search runs entirely in your browser; search terms are not transmitted. There are no
forms and no user accounts.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

The website is served via GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr.
Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When the pages are accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary
access data, in particular the IP address, date and time, the page accessed, and browser
and system information, in order to deliver the pages and to ensure the security of the service.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in the secure and
reliable provision of the website. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub
is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission,
Art. 45 GDPR). Details: [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The operator of this website has no access to this access data and does not analyze it.

## Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, the information you provide is used only to process your inquiry
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for that purpose.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR).
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example
the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.
